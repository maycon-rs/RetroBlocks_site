Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Retro Blocks.

How does Retro Blocks work?

Retro Blocks flashes a pattern on the handheld screen accompanied by distinct chiptune sounds. Your goal is to memorize the sequence and tap the blocks back in the exact order. Each round adds a new step to the pattern!

What is included in the Full Version Unlock?

The Full Version is a one-time non-consumable In-App Purchase. It unlocks the expanded 4x4 and 5x5 boards, multiplayer modes, and guarantees all future updates with zero ads or subscriptions.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Retro Blocks, navigate to the Settings or Unlock screen, and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID, your full unlock will reactivate immediately for free.

Does the game require an internet connection?

No! Retro Blocks is 100% playable offline. Internet is only required if you want to sync scores to Apple Game Center or make an In-App Purchase.
