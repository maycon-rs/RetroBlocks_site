Privacy Policy

Transparent Privacy & User Choice

Retro Blocks ("the App") is committed to total transparency regarding user privacy. While the core game logic does not require user accounts or collect personal contact details, the free version of the App integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements and supports Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Players can easily opt out of tracking or remove all ads permanently with a single In-App Purchase.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Retro Blocks does not require account creation, logins, or access to sensitive device features. We do not directly collect, store, or sell:

  • Your real name, physical address, or phone number
  • Precise GPS location data
  • Contacts, address books, or photo library
  • Microphone, camera, or biometric facial/fingerprint data

2. Advertising (Google AdMob) & App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

The free version of Retro Blocks displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect certain data from your device as outlined in our App Store Privacy Nutrition Label:

  • Identifiers (Device ID / IDFA): Collected for Third-Party Advertising purposes. Not linked to your personal identity.
  • Usage Data (Product Interaction & Advertising Data): Information such as ad impressions, clicks, and gameplay interactions to deliver and measure ad performance. Not linked to your personal identity.
  • Diagnostics (Crash Data): Technical logs and performance metrics to ensure app stability and prevent technical errors. Not linked to your personal identity.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS 14.5+)

When you first launch the app, you will be presented with Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt (NSUserTrackingUsageDescription).

• If you tap "Allow": Google AdMob will use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) to serve personalized ads tailored to your interests.
• If you tap "Ask App not to Track": The IDFA is zeroed out, and Google AdMob will only serve non-personalized, contextual advertisements. You can change this choice at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

For more details on Google’s privacy practices, visit Google Advertising & Privacy Policy.

3. Ad-Free Option via In-App Purchase

Players who prefer an entirely ad-free experience can purchase the Full Version Unlock via Apple In-App Purchase. Purchasing the unlock permanently removes all third-party advertisements and disables ad-related tracking immediately. All payment processing is securely handled directly by Apple through StoreKit without exposing financial data to us.

4. On-Device Data Storage

Game progress, high score streaks, sound preferences, and unlock status are stored locally on your device sandbox via Apple’s standard UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to sync scores with Apple Game Center.

5. Apple Game Center

Retro Blocks supports Apple Game Center for global leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is operated entirely by Apple Inc. under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your Apple ID, passwords, or personal credentials. You can enable or disable Game Center in your device settings.

6. Customer Support Inquiries

When you submit an inquiry through our support form on this website, your name, email, topic, and message are securely recorded via Google Apps Script strictly to respond to your technical questions or feedback. We never sell, share, or use support contact information for promotional marketing.

7. Children’s Privacy

Retro Blocks is designed for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). In accordance with Google AdMob policies and COPPA, ad requests can be flagged for child-directed treatment where applicable.

8. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Updated policies will always be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
