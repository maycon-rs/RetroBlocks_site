Zero Personal Data Collection Policy

Retro Blocks ("the App") is designed with absolute privacy at its foundation. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, or tracking metrics. Everything happens locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Retro Blocks does not require user registration, email sign-ups, or login credentials. The App operates independently and does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • Precise or approximate geolocation data
  • Device Advertising Identifiers (IDFA or IDFV)
  • Audio recordings, microphone input, or camera access
  • Browsing history, contacts, or third-party app analytics

2. On-Device Data Storage

Game data—such as high scores, unlocked board sizes, audio preferences, and settings—is saved locally on your device via Apple standard local sandbox storage (UserDefaults). This data is completely private to your device and is never shared with us or any external third-party servers.

3. Apple Game Center

Retro Blocks integrates with Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is operated entirely by Apple Inc. under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your Apple ID, password, or private account details. You can disable Game Center at any time within your device's iOS / iPadOS Settings.

4. In-App Purchases (StoreKit)

All in-app purchases (such as unlocking the full version) are processed directly by Apple through the official Apple App Store StoreKit framework. We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, billing addresses, or payment credentials. All transactions are governed by Apple's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

5. Support & Customer Inquiries

If you contact us for customer support through our website contact form or directly via email, we only use the provided information (name, email address, message) to troubleshoot your issue and respond to your inquiry. We never share, sell, or use your contact details for promotional mailing lists.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliant)

Retro Blocks is suitable for players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Union). Because the App does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR regulations.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory guidelines. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
